## Status legend

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `covered` | At least one finding maps to this control and satisfies it — no action needed beyond evidence retention. |
| `partial` | Some but not full coverage — a finding maps but doesn't fully satisfy the control's requirement, or coverage exists for part of the in-scope surface only. |
| `gap` | No finding maps to this control and it IS applicable — needs remediation. Feeds the remediation-plan artifact. |
